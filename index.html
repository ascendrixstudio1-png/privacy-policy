01Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways when you use our Services:

Information you provide to us

  • Player name — the name you type when you first use Online Mode. It is shown to other players (in rooms, above your character and in in-game messages) and saved with your online player account. You can change it at any time in the game.
  • Voice — when you are in an online room with your microphone on, your voice is transmitted live to the other players in that room (see Online Play, Voice Chat & Friends).
  • Communications — messages, feedback, support enquiries, or bug reports you send us, including the email address you send them from.
  • Purchase requests — when you choose to buy additional in-app content, your purchase is initiated and processed by the platform store (such as the Meta Horizon Store). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Information collected automatically

When you play offline, nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else. Your game progress, character look and settings are stored only on your own device.

When you use the online features (Online Mode, friends and invites), our service provider Unity creates an anonymous online player account for your device and processes the technical information needed to connect you to other players, such as your IP address and basic device and connection information. Details are in the next section.

Our applications do not include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software, we do not collect your location, and we do not build profiles of our players.

02Online Play, Voice Chat & Friends

The online features of Vice Miami are provided through Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies, which acts as our service provider. These features are used only if you choose to use them. Once you have used Online Mode, the game signs in to these services when it starts, so that invitations from your friends can reach you.

  • Online player account — an anonymous player identifier created by Unity Authentication for your device. It is not linked to your Meta account, your real name or your email address.
  • Multiplayer rooms — to put you in a room with other players (up to 4), the room (Unity Multiplayer Services / Relay) processes your player identifier, your player name, the look of your in-game character, and your IP address and connection data. During a match, your character's movements and in-game actions are shared with the other players in your room. This data exists only for the duration of the room.
  • Voice chat — provided by Unity Vivox. With your permission to use the microphone, your voice is streamed live to the other players in your room so they can hear you. We do not record or store voice chat. You can turn your microphone off at any time with the MIC button in the room and on your wrist menu, and you can refuse microphone permission and still play and listen.
  • Friends and invitations — provided by Unity Friends. The game keeps a list of players you have recently played with on your own device. If you send or accept a friend request, your friends list, friend requests, online status and the invitations you send (which contain the code of your room and your player name) are stored and processed by Unity Friends so they can be delivered to your friends.

Unity processes this data on our behalf to provide these features. Unity's handling of data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

03Meta Horizon Platform Data

Our applications are distributed through the Meta Horizon Store and use Meta platform features. Where you use our Services on a Meta device or through a Meta account, we access the following data from Meta:

  • In-app purchases and downloadable content — your entitlement records, meaning the list of additional in-app items you have purchased. We use this only to unlock the content you have paid for and to restore it when you reinstall or sign in on another device. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not combine it with data from any other source.

This is the only Meta platform data our applications use. We do not request or access your Meta user identifier, profile name, profile photo, friends list, age group, or any other Meta platform data. The friends and voice chat features of our games do not use your Meta friends or Meta profile.

Our applications also confirm that the copy being played is validly licensed. That check is performed entirely within the Meta SDK and returns only a yes or no answer to the application; it does not give us your identity or any personal data, and no identifier is read, stored, or transmitted to us as part of it.

04How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Connect you with other players in online rooms, show your player name and character to them, and run the shared match.
  • Carry your voice to the other players in your room when your microphone is on.
  • Let you send and receive friend requests and invitations to play.
  • Deliver and restore the in-app content you have purchased.
  • Confirm that your copy of the application is validly licensed.
  • Respond to your support requests and communications.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

05Disclosure Of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Other players — when you play online, the players in your room see your player name and character and hear your voice while your microphone is on. Your friends see your player name and online status.
  • Service providers — Unity Technologies provides our online player accounts, multiplayer rooms, voice chat and friends features, and processes the data described above on our behalf.
  • Platform store operators — the store your application was purchased through (such as the Meta Horizon Store) processes your purchases and holds the entitlement records we rely on. We do not use analytics providers, advertising networks, or data brokers.
  • With your consent — when you have explicitly agreed to the sharing.
  • Legal requirements — when required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.

06Data Retention

We hold very little data, and we state plainly how long each kind is kept:

  • Voice chat — transmitted live only; it is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Multiplayer room data — kept only while the room exists, and discarded when it closes.
  • Online player account, player name and friends — kept by Unity on our behalf for as long as you use the online features, and deleted when you ask us to delete them (see Deleting Your Data) or when we stop offering these features.
  • Recently played players — stored only on your own device and removed when you uninstall the application.
  • Entitlement records — read from the Meta Horizon Store while the application is running, so that the content you have bought is unlocked. We do not store a copy on our own servers and we keep nothing after the session ends; the authoritative record is held by the store.
  • Support emails — kept for up to 24 months from your last message so we can follow up on an ongoing issue, then deleted.
  • Game progress and settings — stored only on your own device, under your control, and removed when you uninstall the application.

Where you ask us to delete your data, we act on that request regardless of how long we would otherwise have kept it. See Deleting Your Data below.

07Deleting Your Data

Every user may request deletion of their personal data, at any time, free of charge, without giving a reason.

This right is available to all users worldwide. It is not limited by your country, region, place of residence, nationality, or by whether any particular data protection law applies to you. There are no exceptions based on geographic location.

How to request deletion

Email ascendrixstudio1@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". You do not need an account with us to make a request, and you do not need to explain why. To help us find your online player account, please include your in-game player name and roughly when you last played online.

What happens next

  • We will acknowledge your request and may ask for information needed only to identify the data in question.
  • We will delete the data within 30 days of receiving your request, including your online player account, player name and friends data held by Unity on our behalf.
  • We will confirm by email once the deletion is complete.
  • We will not charge you, and we will not require you to create an account to make a request.

Data stored only on your device (game progress, settings, character look and recently played players) is removed by uninstalling the application. Purchase and entitlement records held by the platform store (for example, the Meta Horizon Store) are controlled by that platform rather than by us. We will delete our own copies of your data; to remove records held by the store itself, please contact the store operator directly.

08Your Rights & Choices

You can choose not to use Online Mode, in which case none of the online data described above is processed. When you do play online, you can turn your microphone off at any time, refuse microphone permission, change your player name, and decline or remove friends.

In addition to deletion, all users may ask us to access, correct, or export a copy of their personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. These rights are available to every user regardless of location.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ascendrixstudio1@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Where local law grants you additional rights beyond those described here, those rights also apply to you — nothing in this policy is intended to limit them.

09Security Of Your Information

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, and our online features are provided through Unity's secured services. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Online play and voice chat let users communicate with other players and are intended only for users aged 13 and over. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at ascendrixstudio1@gmail.com.
